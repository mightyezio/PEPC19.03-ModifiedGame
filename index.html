The Floor
Is Lava

🔥 2 Teams

Floor is Lava

Players navigate a lava-covered court using only mats as safe zones, while Catchers — locked inside their circles — try to tag them. Cross the court. Don't touch the floor.

🟠 Catchers — Goal

Capture as many Players as possible without leaving your assigned circle.

🏃 Players — Goal

Reach the other side of the court using only the mats, without being caught.

Setup +
  • Mats are scattered around the court to act as safe zones. Players may only step on mats.
  • Circles are spread around the court. Catchers are not allowed to leave their circles.
Catcher Rules +
  • Catchers must keep both feet inside the circle at all times.
  • They may stretch and reach to tag Players — as long as they don't step out.
  • Leaving the circle = 1 warning. Two warnings = frozen for 5 seconds, no tagging allowed.
  • Every 60 seconds, Catchers must rotate to a new circle when signaled.
  • If a Catcher fails to rotate, they are frozen for 5 seconds.
  • At the 2-minute mark, all Catchers freeze for 5 seconds (fatigue rule).
Player Rules +
  • Players may only step on mats. Touching the floor = going back to the Start Zone.
  • Each Player is allowed up to 3 floor touches per round.
  • On the 3rd floor touch, the Player goes to the will be frozen for 15 seconds.
  • Players may jump creatively between platforms.
  • No improvised mobility aids allowed (e.g. handkerchief shoe nets). Speed and skill only.
Scoring & Rounds +
  • Each round lasts a fixed 4 minutes.
  • Players may cross the court multiple times within one round.
  • Each successful crossing = 1 point.
  • After scoring, Players immediately return to the Start Zone.
  • Teams earn points for successful crossings and tags.
  • The team with the higher total score across both rounds wins.
  • The team with the most points at the end of the round wins.
Lava Progression +
  • At 1:30 into the round, one mat is declared “burned.”
  • Burned mats may no longer be stepped on.
  • The referee announces: “THE LAVA IS RISING.”
  • Burned mats are marked visually (cone, tape, or crossed arms).

🏁 4 Teams · 2 per Round

Agawan Base

A Filipino classic reimagined with modified prisoner mechanics, neutral safe zones, and base-guard limits. Pure speed and strategy — no violence allowed.

🏁 Win by Base

Touch or step on the enemy team's base without being tagged.

🔒 Win by Capture

Tag and imprison over half of the opposing team's players.

Hover / tap to zoom
Setup +
  • 4 teams total; 2 teams compete per round.
  • Each team has one base (cone / hoop / marked area) and a jail beside it.
  • A clear centerline divides the two territories.
Tagging Rules +
  • Tagging = one-hand tap only.
  • Two or more players may not simultaneously tag the same opponent.
Prisoner System (Modified) +
  • Tagged inside enemy territory → you become a prisoner and go to jail.
  • One teammate can tap you free — but both must return to your own territory without being tagged.
  • If the rescuer is tagged mid-rescue → both become prisoners.
Safe Zones — New Rule +
  • Each team has one neutral safe spot at center court.
  • Maximum stay: 5 seconds.
  • No tagging allowed inside the safe zone.
Base Guard Limit +
  • Maximum 2 players may guard their own base at once.
  • Exceeding this = warning. Second violation = free run for opponents.
Safety & Fair Play +
  • No pushing, hair-pulling, or any violent contact.
  • Speed and strategy only — wits and agility win the game.
  • Players are safe when on or holding their own base.
