Foreign Game

The Floor Is Lava

Game Overview
  • Two teams: Catchers and Players
  • Players must avoid touching the floor
  • Catchers are restricted to circles
Mechanics & Rules
  • Catchers must keep both feet inside the circle
  • Two violations = frozen for 5 seconds
  • Players may jump creatively
  • No mobile tools allowed
Objectives
  • Players cross the court safely
  • Catchers tag as many players as possible

Laro ng Lahi

Agawan Base (Modified)

Winning Conditions
  • Touch the enemy base safely
  • Capture more than half of the opposing team
Modified Bihag System
  • One-hand tagging only
  • No gang tagging
  • Rescue requires both players to return safely
  • If rescuer is tagged → both are prisoners
New Strategic Rules
  • Neutral safe zone (max 5 seconds)
  • No tagging inside safe zone
  • Only 2 defenders allowed at base
